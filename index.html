SparkyDraw

SparkyDraw is a simple, delightful drawing app designed for children using convertible laptops, tablets, or desktop computers. It remembers your artwork and settings across visits, exports high-resolution PNG images locally, and runs seamlessly in both portrait and landscape modes!

🎨 Left Toolbar

  • Colors: Click any of the 12 color slots to pick your drawing color. Click it again to open the color picker and customize that slot.
  • Brush: Click the brush icon to choose your stroke thickness (from fine details to jumbo marker) with live preview.
  • Reset: Click the red Reset button at the bottom of the dock to restore default colors and brush size.

⚡ Top Toolbar

  • Undo & Redo: Instantly undo (Ctrl+Z) or redo (Ctrl+Y) your strokes.
  • Clear: Wipe the canvas clean to start fresh.
  • Fullscreen: Hide all browser borders and distractions for full-screen drawing (F key).
  • Save PNG: Save your drawing directly to your device as SparkyDraw_YYYYMMDD_HHmmss.png.
